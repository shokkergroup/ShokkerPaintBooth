IMAGE FORGE - drop your art here, it becomes a finish at next FRESH START.

NAMING (forgiving - just get close):
  "Stress Storm.png"           -> finds and REPLACES spectrum_stress_storm
  spectrum_stress_storm.jpg    -> exact id, same thing
  Look up ids/names/categories in IMAGE_FORGE_GUIDE.html (repo root).

Subfolders are just organization for REPLACEMENTS (the id decides),
but for NEW finishes the subfolder PICKS the category (folder name = the
picker category, case/emoji ignored), e.g.
  spectrum shift\spectrum_molten_sky.jpg
A new spectrum_* file at the root auto-joins Spectrum Shift.

SIZE: drop ANY size - files are auto-shrunk to 1024x1024 JPG at next start,
renamed to the exact id, and your original is archived in _originals(originals never ship in the installer).

Paint = your art VERBATIM. The spec map is derived from the image
automatically (stroke-flow gloss + hue-banded sequential ignition).
