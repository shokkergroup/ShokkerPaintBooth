not renderer code, does not mark any ID accepted, and must not be used to bypass

## Closed visual carriers (native 2048 contact)

These are topology bans, not merely failed parameter sets. Do not revive them
for another ID with a palette, density, seed, spec-map, or noise alteration:

- **Macro contour/packet sheet** — Chrysina I1: broad uninterrupted rails and
  black packet gaps.
- **Nearest-course ownership** — Cyan Colony I1: decorative macro patchwork,
  not a continuous wet sheet.
- **Detached short-glyph scatter** — old Claw Rake and Bog Murk I1: causal
  source strokes still read as static/confetti on dark ground.
- **Long transported fibre spaghetti** — Cassowary I1: continuous routes still
  read as decorative cable mass rather than a material cortex.
- **Dense Gray-Scott worm regime** — reserved exclusively for Cyan Colony I2;
  related ring/spot/front regimes are not a new Wilds visual family.
- **Packed ownership-cell / foam field** — Foam Film I1: even with unequal,
  tension-warped nucleation, chambers resolve as dark polygon-cell wallpaper
  and duplicate Dragon Hex Glass.  Do not revive with physical labels, colour,
  density, seed, noise or a separate spec response.
- **Isolated puncture-glyph field** — Ground Beetle I1: dense deterministic
  punch/rim/short-gouge marks clump into dark islands and repeat as glyphs,
  rather than resolving as a continuous elytral cortex.  Do not revive through
  rate, placement, colour, seed, threshold, noise or spec changes.
- **Sparse trabecular-tree field** — Antler Bone I2: deterministic osteogenic
  trees with causal cuffs, lacunae and bridges still read as detached Y/rod
  glyphs over black, not close-cropped cancellous bone.  Do not revive by
  adding rate, tint, seed, threshold, noise or an independent spec map.

The pass criterion is not that a construction has semantic names or a distinct
equation. Its uncolored 2048 contact must read as the named material, cover the
sheet as intended, and be visually separate from every survivor before specs
or picker/runtime work begins.
