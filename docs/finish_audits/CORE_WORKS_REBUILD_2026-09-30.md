## What changed in the rebuilding method

Claude’s ASTRA work demonstrated the difference between an independent material/process design and a decorated shared carrier. This rebuild begins with each finish’s visible anatomy and named material masks. General coordinate/noise math is shared; there is no universal decorative carrier or spec enrichment deck. Local metalness, roughness and clearcoat tiers follow the actual named feature. Fine adjacent colour relationships are chosen for the finish, rather than applying the same rainbow everywhere.

Direct comparison remains necessary. The first full actual-output gate had **0 automatic rejects but 5 owner-review flags**. With the owner asleep, those constructions were rebuilt: Flow became short split foil streamlets; Ceramic became intact levelling tongues and attached glass contacts; Air Release became curved adhesive manifolds with oblique blind vents. Separate neighbour inspection caught a Truchet quarter-arc copy that passed the metric; it was replaced with binary triangular foil facets, engraved grip cuts and adhesive windows. Protected Truchet Glass was preserved.

The final native before/after page caught another weakness: Keeled Viper had sparse marks over bare green. Three further retained attempts produced dense overlapping spear scales, corrected front-to-back occlusion so tips stay exposed, and independently bound cool foil faces and warm rough pigment faces. The fourth attempt was selected. A named material promise was checked against actual per-face masks, not accepted merely because it appeared in the contract.

Ceramic glaze, protective film, dry cloth, blackout and translucent shed materials remain restrained where the idea requires it. A low metalness standard deviation is not automatically a defect in a dielectric glaze. Intricacy and meaningful material variety matter; every finish is not forced into the same contrast or metallicity.

## Where to focus the next pass

Use the before/after review to identify visual priorities first. Judge name truth and carrier silhouette with titles hidden, then inspect the native feature density and the material-channel story. Mark quiet designs as plain on purpose when they are doing their job. For bold designs, look for complementary neighbouring details and readable crowns/roots instead of asking for larger marks. Owner “Rebuild” notes should drive the next attempts; a numerical pass cannot override them.

The useful measurement system is a set of intent-aware checks: **name fidelity, material fidelity, fine-detail density, readability across scales, construction independence and actual delivery**. Paint colour population and material-state spread are diagnostics within that intent. They are not universal rankings: a ceramic coat and an electric conductor network should not have the same target distribution.
