👋 Welcome to SHOKK DROP

Turn any image into a race-ready paint finish — with the metallic / roughness / clearcoat “spec” maps that make it shine in iRacing. Drop art, and SHOKK builds the finish for you.

1
Pick a Type on the left (Paint, Pattern, Spec overlay, Paint + spec set, or Car template). Not sure? Leave it on Paint finish.
2
Drop or choose an image in the sidebar (PNG / JPG / WebP). It’s auto-sized to fill the 2048×2048 car canvas.
3
Preview, then Commit — or hit ⚡ SHOKK THE WORLD to explode one image into 20 spec variations and keep your favorites.
4
Use it in the Booth — your drops appear in the SHOKK DROP group inside the Paint Booth, ready to paint your car.

What each import Type does

  • Paint finish — your image becomes the car color; SHOKK auto-generates a matching spec map (the shine). Best starting point. Or attach your own M/R/CC maps below for an exact shine.
  • Pattern — a repeating/overlay design (keeps transparency) you can stamp onto zones. Attach your own M/R/CC maps below to author the shine yourself.
  • Spec overlay — a shine-only layer (no color); your RGB plate maps R→Metallic, G→Roughness, B→Clearcoat. Attach a combined RGB or separate M/R/CC maps below.
  • Paint + spec maps (exact) — you supply BOTH the paint and your own spec maps (combined RGB or separate M/R/CC). Saved verbatim (no auto bake) so it renders exactly as you authored it.
  • Car template (whole car) — a full-body paint+spec that drops over the entire car as one zone.
