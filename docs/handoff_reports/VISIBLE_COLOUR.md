## FINAL - for the owner (plain words)

**What was wrong**
On your 8-zone seafoam design, "make the yellow purple" turned the whole body purple. The yellow body paint is still in the picture you loaded, but your "Seafoam Chalk" zone paints over all of it. The built-in designer looked at the loaded picture, not at what the car shows, so it found "yellow" under the seafoam. It then put a purple zone on top of your seafoam. The online AI had the same blind spot.

**What changed (built-in designer, online AI and MCP alike)**
- "A colour on the car" now means the colour you SEE. The app checks which zones paint over each colour and which zone wins where they overlap (the higher zone wins).
- If the colour shows nowhere, nothing changes. Instead you get: "I do not see any yellow on the body of the car now: your zone “Seafoam Chalk” paints over it, so that part of the car shows mint green. The only yellow you can still see is in the logos / decals ... There is yellow in the original paint under your Seafoam Chalk zone: change that?" It comes with three buttons:
  - "Change the hidden yellow under “Seafoam Chalk”" (or just say "yes"). This does what the old version did, but only because you chose it.
  - "Make the mint green purple". This recolours the seafoam zone itself.
  - "Make all the yellow purple, including the logos".
- If only part of a colour is hidden, only the part you can see changes. The new zone goes just under the zone that covers the rest. Tested with seafoam on the left side only: the left side stays seafoam and the rest turns purple.
- "What colours do I have?" now lists what you see, with zone names: mint green (“Seafoam Chalk”, 44%), hot pink (“GTA Pink”…), and then "most of the yellow (under “Seafoam Chalk”)".
- The AI's describe_paint now reports the visible colours with zone names. Its refinish answers "not visible" and lets the app ask you. Its "X is untouched" claims are now checked against what you saw.
- Your plain PSD (no zones) works exactly as before: 94% of the yellow turns purple, the logos stay yellow, and snakeskin goes in the same zone.
- The server's DSML fix (from FIRSTTEST) is now proven live: DeepSeek wrote a tool call as text, and the server turned it into a real call.
