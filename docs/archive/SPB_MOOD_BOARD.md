## Quick Decision Matrix

Stuck? Pick two moods that almost-but-don't contradict. The resulting livery has tension:

| Mood A | Mood B | Result |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Aggressive | Corporate | Suit-and-knife energy |
| Stealth | Minimalist | Monolith |
| Retro | Loud | 70s disco racer |
| Earth | Corporate | Outdoor-brand flagship |
| Futuristic | Zen | Concept-car clean |
| Loud | Aggressive | Short-track hero |
| Flashy | Retro | Lowrider show car |

**Don't cross more than two moods.** Three or more = confusion.

---

## Usage Tips

- **Write down the mood before you start.** Your future self stays on track.
- **Pick the palette first, then the finishes that match.** Not the other way around.
- **If you hate the result, swap the finish — not the color.** The mood almost always lives in the finish.
- **Revisit the mood board mid-build.** If your car drifted to a different mood than you started, decide: do you fight back, or commit to the new mood?
