## Support: the iRacing User ID (Customer ID) and why a wrong one means "nothing shows"
Shokker's **iRacing User ID** field is your iRacing **Customer ID**, 4 to 7 digits (helmet icon top right in iRacing, then Profile; also the members site, My Account). It is NOT the car number. iRacing matches a paint to a driver by this number inside the file name, so a file with another ID paints THAT driver's car, never yours, and iRacing says nothing. Shokker blocks a render when the field is empty or not 4 to 7 digits ("Enter your iRacing User ID (4-7 digits)..."). A wrong but valid ID renders fine and produces files iRacing never loads for you. Shokker can read the ID from files already in your iRacing paint folders (/api/iracing-id-detect; it fills an empty field by itself after a moment when it is confident). Loading a project or session file can overwrite the ID and the car folder with the saved ones, so check them after loading someone else's file.

## Support: Custom Number vs Sim-Stamped Number and iRacing's Hide Car Numbers setting
There is no field to type a car number in Shokker. The two switches next to the ID only choose the FILE NAME: **Custom Number** writes car_num_<ID>.tga (the paint carries its own number); **Sim-Stamped Number** writes car_<ID>.tga (iRacing stamps the number and sponsors on top). iRacing decides which one to LOAD with its own setting, Settings > Graphics > **Hide Car Numbers**: ON loads car_num_<ID>.tga, OFF (default) loads car_<ID>.tga. They must agree, or iRacing finds no file and silently shows the paint-shop colours. Restart iRacing after changing the setting; iRacing updates can switch it back. Safest when unsure: render once in each mode, both files stay in the folder. The spec is always car_spec_<ID>.tga. Seeing the number twice means a number-stamp layer of the template was left visible when rendering. (Fixed 2026-10-02: the Custom Number switch used to snap back to ON at every launch because /status did not carry the saved value; with an older engine it can still do that until Shokker is restarted once.)

## Support: where iRacing looks, and the car folder field
iRacing reads a car's paint from Documents\iRacing\paint\<car folder>\. The car folder is created by iRacing the first time you run that car in a race or test session. Its name is the car's path with a space instead of the slash, for example "stockcars chevyss"; hovering the tool-tip in iRacing's paint shop shows the exact folder. Shokker's **iRacing Car Folder** must be that car's FOLDER (not a .tga, not Documents\iRacing\paint itself, not a helmets/suits folder). The car menu next to the field lists the folders iRacing created, newest first (newest is not always YOUR car: check the name). Quirk: if the typed folder does not exist but its parent does, Shokker silently saves into the PARENT and reports success, so a typo in the last word means the files land where iRacing never reads them. If Documents is redirected or synced by OneDrive, make sure Documents\iRacing\paint is the one iRacing really uses. A paint for car A in car B's folder is ignored.

## Support: how files get into iRacing (car folder, Auto-deploy, Deploy Now, ZIP)
With **iRacing Car Folder** set, every RENDER copies the paint (car_num_<ID>.tga or car_<ID>.tga) and car_spec_<ID>.tga into it (it also leaves spec_*.tga, paint_base.tga and ORIGINAL_* backups of files it replaced: harmless). **Auto-deploy after render** (Settings gear) only matters when the car folder is EMPTY: then it copies into the last car Shokker remembered. With no car folder and no Auto-deploy car, nothing is copied (banner "No iRacing folder set"). **Deploy Now** under the render sends the LAST render to another car's folder; only the two newest renders are kept ("Job not found" otherwise). **Export ZIP Package** builds a ZIP you download from a link under the render; it is never copied into iRacing. Only RENDER writes into iRacing: live preview and auto-render never do. Paint size must be 2048x2048 (or 1024x1024): other sizes are ignored by iRacing.

## Support: making iRacing show the new paint (reload)
After a render: Alt+Tab to iRacing, in a session with your car, press **Ctrl+R** (Reload Car Textures). The car flashes white, then shows the files on disk. No restart is needed for a new paint or spec. In a replay, move to a moment when your car is not in the pit lane (iRacing does not reload cars in their pit stalls). The 3D Car Viewer (My Content) reloads a saved paint by itself. A restart IS needed after changing Hide Car Numbers or the 2048 paint textures option. If a car folder did not exist yet, run the car once, then copy/render again. Replays always show whatever files are on disk now.

## Support: checklist when "my paint does not show in iRacing" (most likely first)
1 The iRacing User ID is not your Customer ID. 2 The number switch and iRacing's Hide Car Numbers disagree (only one of car_ / car_num_ exists and iRacing wants the other). 3 The car folder is wrong: other car, the paint root, a typo (parent fallback), not created yet. 4 Nothing was copied: no car folder set, or the render failed (read the red banner). 5 No reload: Ctrl+R, replay pit stall. 6 Wrong size (not 2048x2048 / 1024x1024) or not a real TGA: iRacing silently shows paint-shop colours. 7 Black or partly missing paint: re-export 24-bit TGA (a template alpha channel can blank the paint); glass cars need 32-bit with the glass alpha; check the "2048 paint textures" graphics option and restart. 8 Trading Paints Downloader running with "Update My Own Paints" on may swap files in the same folder: close it while testing. 9 Others cannot see it: iRacing never shares textures; they need your files (Trading Paints). Always read the live settings first (check_setup / the Talk to Shokker checklist) instead of guessing.

## Support: the paint looks different in iRacing than in Shokker
- Chrome or metal looks dark or flat: the paint under a metallic part must be near WHITE (iRacing multiplies paint by metal); spec needs metallic ~255 (red) and roughness near 0 (green); blue 255 is dull. Flat chrome on an overcast track can look grey.
- No shine at all: car_spec_<ID>.tga missing or older than the change; Ctrl+R. iRacing compiles it to a .mip; if the shine still looks old, move car_spec_<ID>.mip out of the folder and reload.
- Template lines or masks on the car: Wire, Mask, Car_Mandatory or the "Turn Off Before Exporting TGA" group were visible when rendering. Switch them off and render again.
- Number or sponsors twice, or sparkly: iRacing stamps its own numbers and sponsors; the stamp layers must be off, and stamped items take the material of whatever the spec has under them.
- Preview vs sim: the preview is a quick flat view; iRacing adds sun, shadows, materials and stamps.

## Support: the RENDER button will not start (guards, in the order the app checks them)
"A render is already running" (wait). Button reads OPEN PAINT FIRST / LOADING PAINT (open the template and wait). "Paint engine is offline - it restarts automatically" (wait 10 s, else reopen the app). "Set the Source Paint path" / "Source Paint needs a FULL path" (use the PSD/XCF/ORA or TGA/PNG/JPEG button, or paste the full path). "Enter your iRacing User ID (4-7 digits)". "To render: pick a color on the paint (Pick + Add), then assign a Finish ... Both are required" (a zone needs a colour AND a finish; muted zones are skipped; it also appears when a zone is limited to a hidden or missing layer). The button is never greyed for missing zones: it shows the toast. A stale imported spec map with no valid zone ends in "No valid zones". After a Spec Sculpt save, main RENDER keeps that spec and ignores zones for shine: clear the imported spec map in Settings.

## Support: render errors and what they mean
**render_busy**: one render at a time; Terminate only stops the app WAITING, the engine still finishes and still copies the files into iRacing; wait a minute. **Render server unreachable / engine offline / hung**: the engine restarts itself; if not, close and reopen the app; antivirus may block python.exe. **Timed out after 5 minutes**: too heavy, use fewer zones. **aggregate RLE decode budget exceeded / decode failed / out of memory**: more than about 16 zones with their own painted area (256 MB of masks); undo or start over. **OUTPUT FOLDER ERROR / iRacing output folder not found / No active car configured**: fix the car folder. **Failed to push ... PermissionError / Copy verification failed**: another program holds the TGA (iRacing paint preview, image viewer): close it and render again. **Unknown base/pattern id ... Did you mean**: a retired finish in a zone; pick another. **Finish Pack needed**: the finish lives in a pack that is not installed (download it if your version has a Finish Packs screen, then restart), or pick another finish. **cross_origin_forbidden**: use the Shokker window, not a hand-opened browser tab. **Job not found**: only the 2 newest renders are kept. Console and logs: Settings > Report a Problem builds a redacted report.

## Support: the live preview is stuck, blank or stale
Press F5 (or click the red "Preview failed - showing the LAST GOOD render" bar). It fails when the engine is offline, a zone has a huge mask set (about 16 zones with their own painted area is the limit), or a decode error occurs; it retries by itself ("Server busy - retrying", "Refreshing preview source"). Preview times out after 18 s and retries a faster one. A different PSD opened with the old preview still showing means the preview source did not refresh: F5. The preview is not the render and never writes files.

## Support: Trading Paints, other drivers, and sharing
iRacing never sends textures between drivers; others see your paint only if your files are in THEIR paint folder, which the Trading Paints service and its Downloader do. Shokker only writes local files (a Copy TP Desc button copies a text description; no upload). The Downloader writes into the same folders with the same names: while testing a local paint, close it or turn off "Update My Own Paints" (whether it overwrites a local file is not documented: say "may"). Trading Paints calls car_ files Sim-Stamped Number paints and car_num_ files Custom Number paints (Custom Number needs TP Pro). Always give the car a plain paint-shop colour scheme in iRacing so others do not see a white car. OneDrive on Documents\iRacing can break the Downloader.

## Support: the spec file and the .mip
car_spec_<ID>.tga is optional. Red = metallic, green = roughness (0 mirror, 255 rough), blue = clearcoat (0 off, 16 very shiny, 255 dull), alpha = lighting mask. 24-bit, or 32-bit with alpha. iRacing compiles it to car_spec_<ID>.mip the first time it loads it: for your own car it reads the .tga when that is newer than the .mip, then rebuilds the .mip, so Ctrl+R is enough after a render; other drivers need the .mip (Trading Paints shares it). When the spec is final you can delete the spec .tga so iRacing stops rebuilding it at every start. Numbers and sponsors that iRacing stamps take the material of whatever the spec holds under them.

## Support: limits and things Shokker does not do
Cars only: helmet and suit folders are not Shokker targets in this build. No automatic Trading Paints upload. Terminate does not stop the engine. Report a Problem does not include your ID or number mode (ask for them, or run the Talk to Shokker check). Shokker cannot read iRacing's own settings (Hide Car Numbers, 2048 paint textures): ask the buyer to look. It cannot see what the sim shows: ask what they see (white car, paint-shop colours, old paint, black parts) before concluding. The AI never renders, saves, exports or deletes for the buyer.

## Support: how to help a buyer with a render or iRacing problem (for AIs)
1 Call check_setup FIRST (in-app) or spb_check_setup (MCP): it reads their live settings and the files in their car folder and returns findings. 2 Tell them ONE or two likely causes in plain words, most likely first, using the findings; do not dump the whole list. 3 Give exact clicks (top bar, Settings gear, Layers panel). 4 Ask for the exact message when there is one; match it against "Support: render errors". 5 Say which side a step is on (Shokker or iRacing) and that Hide Car Numbers and Ctrl+R are iRacing side. 6 Never invent file names, menu items or iRacing options that are not on these cards. 7 Offer the one-click fixes the checklist card has (ID from folder, number mode switch, open car menu, template layers off). 8 When it works, tell them to render again and press Ctrl+R.

## Support: where the settings are
Top bar: iRacing User ID, Custom Number / Sim-Stamped Number, Source Paint (PSD/XCF/ORA and TGA/PNG/JPEG buttons), iRacing Car Folder (car menu and folder button), RENDER, SPEC SCULPT. Settings gear (top right): Auto-deploy after render, Export ZIP Package, Import Spec Map, Report a Problem, License (render does not need a license check). Under the render: Deploy Now (to another car), Save to keep, Download ZIP Package. Layers panel: template layers (Wire, Mask, Car_Mandatory). Chat: the helper answers questions and checks settings; the gear in the chat sets an AI key and the Brain (Claude or OpenAI models through OpenRouter).
