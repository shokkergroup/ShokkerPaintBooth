# IRIDESCENT INSECTS — 50-card rebuild ledger

Owner goal, 2026-09-01: rebuild the category as 50 hand-authored, borderless, whole-car materials. Each card must have its own visual grammar and its own independently phased metallic / roughness / clearcoat logic. This is a design ledger, not a recipe generator permission.

## Non-negotiable delivery rules

- Native carrier marks stay predominantly 8–32px at 2048². Larger organisms are never stamped silhouettes: their **microstructure** fills the canvas.
- Every card uses at least five purposeful mark families (for example ribs, seams, pore rims, glints, scratches, lamellae), and no random-confetti/grain rescue.
- No borders, frames, one-off centered art, or palette-only variants. A card must remain coherent when scaled down or cropped over a car panel.
- Spec is literal `M / Rough / Cc`, with 8–12 or more named material states distributed through the same carrier rather than a one-field recolor.
- First release batches remain live-development visible only after native/card/picker evidence, M7 where supported, and root→Electron parity.

## Research anchors used

- **Morpho ridge systems:** stacked lamellae and ridge irregularity make broad-angle blue flash possible; use parallel fine ridge/buttress grammar, not a blue gradient. [J. Vac. Soc. Japan, 2009](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jvsj2/52/4/52_4_218/_article/-char/en)
- **Jewel-beetle elytra:** treat reflective shell as a photonic-crystal cuticle, so microfacets and depth parcels are the carrier rather than rainbow noise. [Optica proceedings](https://opg.optica.org/abstract.cfm?uri=LAOP-2014-LTu4A.33)
- **Dragonfly wings:** veins and cross-veins are structural, with membrane and resilient vein contrasts; use fine load-path meshes with different material states. [PMC study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4785991/)
- **Firefly lantern:** a photogenic layer, reflector and nanostructured cuticle motivate ordered extraction prisms inside a dark shell rather than painted yellow dots. [PMC study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3503230/)
