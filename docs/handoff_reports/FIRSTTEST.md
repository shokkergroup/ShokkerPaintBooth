## FINAL - for the owner (plain words)

**What was wrong**
1. The built-in designer gave up because it read "reflective snakeskin pattern" as ONE unknown finish name (the catalogue has none called that). It never knew a texture can ride along with a colour change. ("I want to", "some type of", "give it" were never the problem.)
2. The online AI was allowed to recolour "the yellow" everywhere, so the yellow in your logos, spray cans and Shokker logo went purple too.
3. The AI used TWO zones for one job (colour in one, snakeskin in another). Only the top zone shows on shared pixels, so one of the two always vanished. In your run a "Yellow metallic" zone sat on top of the purple: the body stayed yellow, and the purple showed only on edges and darker golds.
4. The purple lines on your left-side white stripes came from an "AI zone" the model put on the White Base layer (those stripes live on that layer). I reproduced your exact picture: body yellow, purple stripes.
5. The AI was allowed to undo its own half-done work in the middle of a reply. The reply was its own text ("Done ...") and never a check of the car, so it said "Done" over a yellow car. DeepSeek sometimes writes tool calls as text (the `<｜DSML｜...>` junk), and that went straight into the chat.

**What changed**
- Built-in designer: your sentence and its variants ("make the yellow purple with a snakeskin shine", "turn the yellow into purple metallic snake scales", "yellow -> purple chrome with a reptile texture") are now ONE change, at no cost. It is a single zone on the Yellow Base layer: purple keeping the shading, metallic (or chrome), plus the Snake Scale Diamond shine texture. One Undo takes it back. The reply says the snakeskin is in the shine (sim / spec preview). It offers two chips: "...with a snakeskin print" (drawn in the paint instead) and "Also the yellow in the logos and numbers".
- The body-paint rule is now enforced inside the add / edit zone handlers, so the built-in designer, the AI and MCP all obey it. Naming numbers, logos or decals lifts it.
- The AI's `refinish` now takes a `texture`, so colour + finish + texture go into one zone. A second zone on the same pixels gets merged into the first. The AI can no longer undo its own work unless you asked for an undo.
- After every pass, the reply is rebuilt from what is on the car NOW, measured on the preview. It checks whether the asked colour is still there, whether it turned into the new colour, whether the texture zone shows, and whether anything else changed. If the job is not done, the reply says "Not done yet: ..." with Try again / Do it offline / Undo, never "Done". The "What changed" line lists only zones that are really there.
- The DSML junk is stripped in the app. The server also turns well-formed DSML into real tool calls (needs a server restart).
