Proposed direction · not yet implemented

The Bases picker. Ten coherent families.

Use the same category tiles, navigation, search, favorites and selection behavior as Bases. Give spec overlays their own catalog adapter and accurate surface/applied-spec previews.

Organize by construction. Use matte/gloss, metal contrast, coat-only, subtle reveal and theme tags as cross-cutting filters. Preserve legacy recipe IDs and rendering behavior through an explicit versioned path.

Twelve proofs before a bigger catalog.

Design briefs only. Each needs researched references, its own identity contract, native render evidence and real-car review. Fine features remain 8–32px at 2048².

A planning target of approximately 120 accepted constructions across these families would be meaningful. The count follows the quality and identity gates; a recolor or shared carrier earns no extra slot.
