## Butter Whorl I2 design brief

### Required carrier

Build a full-bleed fractured-lacquer sheet whose local calyx arms and ratchet shoulders interlock across irregular territories. It should read first as premium automotive coating, then reveal the catch mechanics on inspection.

The large-scale carrier should be unequal fractured lacquer territories or staggered catch corridors—not circles, rows, tiles, a repeated radial glyph, or a global vortex. Local arm segments can curve and hook, but their ownership must be distributed across the connected sheet.

### Required attached anatomy

Use at least five visibly distinct, causally attached families, for example:

1. unequal lacquer bays or calyx plates,
2. hooked catch arms and groove shoulders,
3. broken rim teeth and interlock notches,
4. snapped tips, scars, or repaired fracture cuts,
5. fine 8–32 px inner lamellae/brush histories,
6. narrow exposed optical lips,
7. button/collar pressure nodes used irregularly rather than as repeated centers,
8. short abrasion ticks or foil microcuts attached to the same structures.

Do not add these as decorative layers floating over unrelated paint. They must describe one material history.

### Color and optical response

- Ground: black or near-black lacquer sink, used to isolate small high-value features.
- Body: butter/cream/old-gold territories with enough separation to read as material depth rather than a flat yellow fill.
- Visible highlight lips: butter/yellow, pink, violet, white, and controlled blue.
- Green: exclude from visible highlight intervals for this concept.
- Do not copy Oil Slick's rainbow order.
- A/B must preserve topology while moving which fractures, teeth, collars, and foil lips are optically exposed.

### Causal material maps

- **Metalness:** exposed foil within teeth, snapped tips, worn collars, and thin fracture lips.
- **Roughness:** groove interiors, snapped cuts, abrasion, and fractured troughs.
- **Clearcoat:** intact lacquer bays, smoother catch shoulders, and pooled/order-transition surfaces.

M/R/Cc should be independently caused grayscale maps with broad multi-tier ranges. They may correlate where the physical story overlaps, but must not be copied, inverted copies, or unrelated score-chasing fields.
