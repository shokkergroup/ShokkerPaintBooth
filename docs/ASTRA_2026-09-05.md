# ASTRA — ten material constructions

Owner request, 2026-09-05: build the best ten finishes we can, in a new **ASTRA** category. Local work reference **SPB-105 / ASTRA**, ticks A1 onward. This is an artistic and engineering effort, not a forecast of sales.

## What the existing system teaches

- `engine/registry.py` supplies the V5 picker, while `shokker_engine_v2.py` also installs bases into the full renderer. Both must receive new finishes. A BASE needs a `paint_fn` and `base_spec_fn`; the same deterministic geometry must drive both.
- Standard thumbnails and buyer split cards have different render paths. Final proof must use `rebuild_thumbnails.py`, `rebuild_picker_swatches.py` and the actual served assets. Source-only proof cannot establish live parity.
- Hologram Metal and the owner-approved Cinder Pulse lesson: dark adjacent pigments, small structured colour islands and multiple material states can create surprising light-dependent changes. Existing glint/mosaic recipes share carriers; ASTRA will not call them or copy their spatial fields.
- Red is metallic, green is roughness. Blue **16 is strongest active clearcoat**, while 255 suppresses it. ASTRA stays in 16..255, avoiding the ambiguous 0..15 path. A colourful packed spec map is not pigment and is not proof of a convincing car.
- The old M7 combines catalog-relative proxies; some historical categories are even hardcoded to 100. ASTRA receives no score override or clone exemption. Render measurements, fine-scale law, independent structural comparison, runtime parity and eye review accompany the score.
- Real displacement, transmission, anisotropic normals, emissive light and physical spectral diffraction cannot be added by these two textures alone. The designs below encode pigment and material contrasts inspired by those processes. They do not claim to implement new iRacing shaders.

## Pre-render identity plan (A1)

Every carrier is authored independently. Shared utilities may pack channels, shade named masks, cache pairs and sample coordinates; they may not supply a common decorative carrier. Native motifs occupy 8–32 pixels at 2048 square. Every feature receives eight brightness/tint tiers and material variation. No category count credit for recolours.

## Verification and release plan

1. Validate each executable `spb-finish-identity/1` contract before metric scoring. Record an agent eye assessment separately; the schema's required pre-render name-test declaration is an intended design assertion, not an owner verdict.
2. Render native 2048 pairs; inspect contact sheets and 1:1 crops. Measure three fresh pair builds per finish against the 3-second budget. Check deterministic seeds, masks, non-square output and bounded cache behavior.
3. Audit paint, combined spec and each individual material channel at native crop, standard and picker scale. Reject transformed structural duplicates at 0.68; 0.55..0.68 requires owner review. Compare nearest existing materials too.
4. Populate measured scorecard rows, run official workbook M1/M2/M5/M6/M7, require all ten M7 >=85, and preserve actual measured failures while iterating. No synthetic perfect scores.
5. Install ASTRA in the live development catalog, bake real assets, synchronize only owned files, verify served bytes and app visibility. Do not release a public installer as part of this task.
6. Provide a concise owner review with full-resolution paint/spec access. Actual same-position sun, shade and night iRacing comparisons remain the authority for commercial appearance claims.

## Build record

- A1: research and contracts written before rendering. M7 baseline **unscored**, not zero. Owner verdict guiding implementation: “the best bases/patterns and specs man has ever seen”; fine detail, many spec shades, unique name-true construction.
- A2: sharpened nine native generators to 2048; changed square-looking Phoenix grain centres into irregular grains. All ten cleared the calibrated fine/follow law. Seven A1 alignment failures became passes by assigning roughness to exposed polish within each feature.
- A3: category similarity rejected Event Horizon / Cryogenic Bloom at **0.695**. Cryogenic Bloom was rebuilt as connected frost ferns, eliminating the shared radial packing silhouette. Pigment separation strengthened within the same small features. No enlargement used.
- Workbook calibration: the initial `full` run scored **63.7–85.4**. M1's tiny dHash views collapse fine fields; M2 interpreted `gold` as a saturation requirement. ASTRA now uses the existing `fine_structural_color` intent, already used by fine Wilds/Neon, with normal clone penalties retained. M6 tests pigment population, three channel ranges and channel independence. Its catalog-relative 3px paint-energy test was removed for this category because old differently sized bakes are not a valid native-resolution comparator. The calibrated **8–32px spectral scale gate remains separately mandatory**, together with geometry, name review, material detail alignment and rendered similarity. No metric formula or perfect-score override was added.
- A4/A5: Phoenix's full-grid nearest-neighbour work took 3.51–3.87s. Precomputed per-grain randomness and 1536 sampling (six samples per 8px feature, antialiased to 2048 output) brought the pair to **1.73–1.81s**. Quasicrystal Crown remained at **83.7 M7** until eight independently coated populations were assigned inside every crystal feature; it reached **96.2**. The third material channel now does independent work.
- A6: **real export exposed a material-binding bug invisible in isolated renders**. One job called ASTRA using seeds 6654, 42 and 4284 for material / source / colour-source paths. The prior adapter produced different texture populations and performed three native builds. ASTRA's public adapters now use a fixed authored seed 42, while direct `build` remains seedable for research. The first rechecked export has **paint MAE 0.0**, packed-spec MAE **0.265 byte** (rounding), and one native build. All ten require the same full-pipeline proof before completion. This change affects ASTRA only.
- Cache freshness: both each finish module and shared ASTRA material utilities are declared through the existing `_spb_picker_dependency_modules` hook. Bakes cannot miss a transitive material change.
- Generated scorecard: preserved every existing record while compacting from **51,000 to 1,990 lines**. Its old line guard now passes. `engine/spec_patterns.py` was already 37,007 lines against 13,880 and remains outside this lane; no unrelated finish output was changed to clear that pre-existing guard.

## Product plan

The credible sales story is a visible result, not a score or a promise of financial rescue. Lead with three very different ASTRA finishes on the **same car, camera and location**, then rotate the sun or compare matched shade/night views. Show the actual one-selection workflow and native detail close-up. Use the other seven as depth in the collection, not ten nearly identical teaser shots. Record the iRacing version, car and conditions with every claim. After owner track review, choose the strongest three for a short storefront demonstration, offer the full ten through the app, and collect buyer results before deciding what to expand. No public listing, installer, price or message was changed in this task.
