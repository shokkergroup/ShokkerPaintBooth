# Shokker Paint Booth — Keyboard Shortcuts Reference Card

**Version:** v6.2.0 "Boil the Ocean"
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-17

Printable cheat sheet of every keyboard shortcut in SPB. Organized by category. Print the relevant section, tape it to your monitor, move faster.

> **Tip:** In the app, press `?` any time to open the live shortcut overlay. `Esc` dismisses.

---

## Conventions

- `Ctrl+X` means hold Control, press X. On macOS substitute `Cmd` where indicated.
- Shortcuts are shown as `Key` or `Modifier+Key`.
- "Context" column means the panel or mode the shortcut applies to. "Global" means anywhere.

## Platform Notes

- **macOS:** substitute `Cmd` for `Ctrl` throughout (SPB follows OS conventions).
- **Linux:** SPB is Windows-first; Linux support via Wine is community-maintained, shortcuts should work identically.
- **Tablet / pen input:** brush shortcuts (`[`, `]`) work identically. Tablet pressure is auto-detected.
