Default: **ten genuinely different designs per requested theme; one 16:9 artist sheet per design; five views of that same car on each sheet**. The established style is polished motorsports concept rendering, with a hero, both profiles, right-rear angle, overhead, title and palette. DLM is this task's default car unless another is named.

The reusable prompt and visual review require:

- DLM roof numbers forward or passenger-facing, never driver-facing. Pick one physical direction per design and keep it consistent.
- Rear-deck graphics normally facing the spoiler, including illustrations and logos, not just text.
- Actual supplied Shokker Paint Booth logo and/or at minimum the SHOKKER hand mark in every design's livery.
- Different compositions and visual ideas across the ten, coherent identity across each sheet's five views, readable numbers, and inspection of every actual panel.

The previous master prompt explicitly requested driver-facing roofs. That instruction is obsolete. Historical sheets and receipts remain preserved as evidence, not certified examples of the new orientation/branding requirements.
