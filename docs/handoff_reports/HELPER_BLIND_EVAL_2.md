## Top 5 failure patterns

1. **Sponsor / logo / number / team-name asks are run through the finish-name search.** Replies like "I do not know a look called sunoco / png / primary / joes garage / both" and a step that targets the SPONSORS layer (b2_053, 055, 057, 064, 065, plus 056/058 sent online). Nothing is applied yet, but it is junk to the buyer and one confirm away from putting a finish on the sponsor layer. Needs a dedicated element path: say plainly what the app can do (recolour an existing number, ask for the image/text) instead of a look picker.
2. **Compound requests are half-parsed and the dropped clauses are silently lost or misassigned.** Colour, part or finish disappears (b2_007 lost quarter panels + blue, 014 lost splitter + black, 018 lost camo/green, 022 retro stripes keeps only blue, 028 chameleon keeps only purple, 031 roof dropped, 049 only the wing parsed), or roles flip (036 "white with a red stripe" recolours white things to red, 043 gunmetal base becomes "recolour gunmetal to gold", 023 two-tone becomes one finish). 17 of 52 DO asks landed in PREFILL with an unresolved target ("?") even when the part was named.
3. **Negation and "keep" are not respected.** "only the body not the numbers" produced a step on the NUMBERS (b2_042, harmful). "gold pinstripe" became "recolour gold things black + matte" (b2_050, harmful, can hit gold numbers/sponsors). Same family: 029/030 got the right protection only by luck of the body-layer route.
4. **Vague or follow-up phrases get a random encyclopedia article instead of a clarifying question.** "less" (spec_strength), "the other one" (mirror recipe), "i want a race car" (wear), "remove it", "red or blue? i cant decide" (flat_or_shiny), "what time is it" (live link), "what can you actually do" (share_everything), "how many finishes" (finish_cards). Only 3 of 23 ASK inputs got an ASK; 9 got a PREFILL with "I do not know a look called other/match".
5. **Art asks and owner vocabulary are matched to the nearest catalogue word and APPLIED.** "dragon breathing fire" auto-applies Dragon Scale to the left side, "airbrush a skull with roses" auto-applies Chain Link on the trunk (b2_127, 134). "crushed down to 50 percent" is read as Brushed Metal (b2_082; owner vocabulary says crushed = scale). Spec-only "satin, dont change the colours" is answered with the base-scale article (b2_011) instead of the Foundation spec-only route; "the shine" variants only work when the sentence is simple.
