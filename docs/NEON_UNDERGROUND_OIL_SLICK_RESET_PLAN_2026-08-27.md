## Decision

Rebuild Neon Underground as **25 premium automotive coating processes**, not as pictures of
neon places, nightlife props, gauges, pipes, flags, or tire donuts.

- Preserve the existing 20 IDs so old saves continue to resolve.
- Rebuild all 20 visual outputs from zero; none of the legacy renderers is a visual keeper.
- Add five new IDs as reversible live-dev entries so the owner can judge them in the app. The
  rejected Claude prototype IDs are not compatibility constraints because they were never wired.
- Apply the Oil Slick discovery to **all 25 at the material-system level**: paint and material
  response must be co-registered and causal.
- Use Oil Slick's visibly electric boundary language on roughly **10–12 of 25**, not all 25.
  The remainder need different responses (glass, velvet, wetting, phosphor, etched metal,
  rubber, ceramic) so the category does not become 25 Oil Slick relatives.

Category sentence: **underground/night/racing energy translated into charged automotive
materials, never literal poster art.**

## What Oil Slick actually discovered

Oil Slick is not successful because it has a rainbow or an RGB spec map. Its raw M, R, and Cc
maps are grayscale. The breakthrough is **co-registration**: one material history controls
the color territories, electric lips, fine ridges, view response, and all three material maps.

1. One continuous sheet is deformed. Nothing is pasted on afterward.
2. Broad thickness territories provide readable hierarchy.
3. Fine 8–26px strain lamellae live inside those territories.
4. Rare shocks, dry valleys, cusps, and optical-order jumps create high-energy accents.
5. View/light movement changes which attached features are exposed; it does not globally hue
   rotate the whole image.
6. M, R, and Cc answer different physical questions about the same anatomy:
   - M: exposed substrate / foil / conductive lips.
   - R: strain, rupture, abrasion, troughs, and damaged microstructure.
   - Cc: intact resin, glassy shoulders, smooth pools, and healed surfaces.

Current Oil Slick evidence:

- causal coverage: film territory 17.77%, fine ridges 25.33%, optical jumps 4.44%, shocks
  0.226%, dry valleys 0.544%, cusps 0.0026%;
- M/R/Cc std: 53.514 / 33.926 / 36.522;
- M/R/Cc range: 6–250 / 14–249 / 5–252;
- eight occupied tiers in every channel;
- pair correlations: +0.264 / -0.272 / +0.056, related but neither copied nor inverse;
- current M7: 89.1;
- complete native proof runs under the 3-second budget.

The packed RGB spec preview looks colorful because it visualizes three independently varied
grayscale channels. That color is evidence of different M/R/Cc triples, not permission to
paint a literal RGB spec texture.

## Why the rejected Neon attempt failed

### Visual failure

- Redline Tach became repeated gauge stamps.
- Boost Spool became cable spaghetti.
- Quarter Mile became giant checker banners and light-tree icons.
- Burnout Ring became annular stamp wallpaper.

Those are illustrations spread over a sheet, not one automotive material process. Their macro
motifs also break the 8–32px doctrine: Redline uses radii up to 162px before diameter/bezel,
Quarter Mile uses 34–72px canvas-spanning ribbons, and Burnout uses 156 annuli up to 165px in
radius.

### Material failure

The prototypes use broad generic FBM/pool fields as spec underlays. In several cases M comes
from one view of that field while R and Cc use inverse or parallel views. Visible motifs receive
some hardcoded channel assignments, but the dominant material motion is not caused by the
dominant paint anatomy. The packed spec sheet therefore looks busy and colorful without reading
as one physical coating.

### Gate failure

The brightness-coverage quota rewards lighting every 256px cell, which incentivizes clutter.
The fineness score measures generic high-frequency energy rather than actual 8–32px authored
primitives. The verifier does not run M7, does not perform the promised cross-catalog gate, and
does not exit nonzero for all printed failures. Its last individual records include 5.76s,
3.30s, and 4.08s failures immediately before a manual aggregate `gates_green` record.

Keep only the useful infrastructure lessons: isolated zero-breakage development, one-pass
paint/spec output, grayscale tier ladders, deterministic caching, bounded distance transforms,
and square-seam detection.

## Architecture for the reset

Do not extend or wire `engine/paint_v2/neon_math_v2.py`.

Use:

- one small shared utility module for deterministic RNG, tier mapping, causal-mask diagnostics,
  glow falloff, scale instrumentation, and evidence writing;
- one independent finish module per finish, each owning its carrier and causal anatomy;
- an explicit result contract containing paint, M/R/Cc, named anatomy masks, and a geometry
  ledger recording authored widths/counts;
- compatibility adapters as part of every live dev promotion, preserving both legacy
  base+monolithic routes for `neon_*` and monolithic/Fusion routes for `neon2_*`.

Shared code may provide math tools. It may not provide a shared carrier, shared spec backdrop,
or palette-swapped composer.

## Five pilot concepts

These were the original pilot concepts. The owner later authorized the remaining slate while
away, then superseded the isolation/no-wiring gate with the live-dev visibility rule.

### 1. Blue Breakdown — `neon_electric_blue`

- Carrier: dark ceramic dielectric lacquer with distributed 8–24px breakdown packets.
- Anatomy: primary fissures, secondary forks, blue-white fused lips, quenched channels,
  violet temper shoulders, corona beads, healed glass bridges.
- M: exposed/fused substrate lips and rupture nodes.
- R: char channels, residue, and heat shoulders.
- Cc: intact ceramic and healed bridges; sharply reduced over ruptures.
- Veto: lightning-bolt silhouettes, Voronoi crackle, neon tubes, or Oil Slick flow contours.

### 2. Sodium Scuff — `neon_orange_hazard`

- Carrier: retroreflective orange microbeads partially embedded in black polyurethane.
- Anatomy: bead clusters, exposed cores, dragged resin tails, crushed shards, polished skid
  lips, deep gouges, path-attached rebound specks.
- M: reflective backing and crushed shards.
- R: gouges/shards high; burnished lips low.
- Cc: intact resin and pooled tails high; exposed tracks low.
- Veto: caution tape, chevrons, polka dots, random confetti, or an asphalt scene.

### 3. Redline Shear — `neon_red_alert`

- Carrier: fluorescent red lacquer compressed into staggered local 8–32px sweep-front packets.
- Anatomy: compression fronts, tick-comb microcuts, white-hot lips, recoil scars, cavitation
  pits, rare sheared micro-check fragments, magenta cooling tails.
- M: exposed aluminum at the highest-pressure lips and cuts.
- R: scars and pits high; burnished fronts low.
- Cc: intact trailing shoulders and pools high; ruptures low.
- Veto: gauges, circles, needles, numerals, checker banners, or recoverable large arcs.

### 4. Quarter-Mile Weave — `neon2_quarter_mile_weave`

- Carrier: fine black/fluorescent textile weave laminated under clear and locally stretched.
- Anatomy: 8–16px warp/weft cells, elongated slip zones, broken threads, timing hairlines,
  staged dot micro-ladders, rubber pickup, resin bridges, reflective stitch knots.
- M: metalized warp and stitch knots.
- R: frays and rubber high; intact resin low.
- Cc: resin bridges and untouched weave high; pickup/fray low.
- Veto: flags, full-width ribbons, Christmas trees, lanes, uniform checker wallpaper, or panels.

### 5. Torque Scar — `neon2_torque_scar`

- Carrier: clear-coated carbon-black elastomer transfer over a metallic dark base.
- Anatomy: short 8–32px tangential shear packets, tread microblocks, heat lips, rubber crumbs,
  glaze islands, cross-shear cuts, smoke-etch whiskers, exposed-clear gaps.
- M: substrate scores and embedded hot grit only.
- R: raw rubber, crumbs, and cuts high; glazed tracks low.
- Cc: intact clear high, glazed transfer mid, raw rubber low.
- Veto: complete circles, mentally completable rings, bubbles, tire illustrations, or floating
  smoke ribbons.

## Required gates

- Most authored primitives are 8–32px at 2048. Geometry instrumentation, not a generic
  high-pass score, proves it.
- At least five visibly distinct, causally attached anatomy families.
- Full-canvas material coverage without a poster composition, repeated stamp field, or macro
  void. Coverage is measured from named causal masks, not brightness.
- Eight occupied response tiers per M/R/Cc, broad ranges, and target channel std >=30
  (absolute floor 20 only with an explicit material reason).
- Per-feature tier variation: a bright line may not receive one constant M/R/Cc triple.
- Copied/inverse-channel detection and a written cause for each map.
- M7 >=85 for every modified/new finish.
- Deterministic native 2048 paint+spec within 3 seconds.
- Edge/seam continuity.
- In-set and whole-catalog color-stripped paint **and spec-topology** uniqueness.
- Exact root/package parity only after promotion.
- All verification commands fail closed with nonzero exit status.
