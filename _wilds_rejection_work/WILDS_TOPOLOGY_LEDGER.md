# Fractured Wilds topology ledger — owner-test catalog

This is a source-level anti-clone contract for the 21 Wilds IDs currently
exposed by the catalog. It is not a quality score and cannot promote a finish.
Every future renderer must name one **new primary carrier** and must not reuse
an occupied or closed carrier merely with another palette/spec recipe.

## Candidate authoring rule

Before a new renderer is written, add a one-line primary mechanism here and
screen raw native topology. The candidate must be full coverage, have visible
8–32px detail, retain a nameable physical story in hue-null, and have five or
more causal mark types. Its M/R/Cc maps must arise from different physical
histories. A candidate that collides with a reserved/closed row is rejected;
changing colours, frequency, density, blur or noise does not create a new
carrier.
